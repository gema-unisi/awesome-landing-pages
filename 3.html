Our Process

From coconut shell to global market.

A controlled production flow designed to turn renewable coconut shell biomass into high-quality charcoal briquettes.

01

Raw Material

Selected coconut shells are collected and prepared.

02

Carbonization

Coconut shells are processed into charcoal.

03

Crushing

Charcoal is crushed and prepared for production.

04

Briquetting

Charcoal powder is mixed, compressed and shaped.

05

Packaging

Finished products are packed and prepared for shipment.
